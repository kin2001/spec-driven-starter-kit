# Delivery handoff

Historical observations, not instructions or authorization. Relay publishes this body with handoff.py; never copy metadata from an older file. Aim for 300-500 words plus evidence links, not a transcript. Keep unresolved facts even when older than the last completed task.

## Relevant contracts and decisions

## Next task

<intended issue and owner; outcome, prerequisites and relevant paths. The owner's named issue selects the target; read its current scope/status/blockers once.>

## Unresolved carryover

<blockers, WIP paths/fingerprints, failed checks, uncertain provider operations and required recovery; explicitly none only with evidence. Do not reset equivalent-failure counts.>

## Ownership at checkpoint

<controller/claim and recorded worker state; all in-flight actions and quiescence evidence. This historical field never transfers ownership.>

## Verification boundary

<when and against which HEAD/base/tree and source/environment inputs facts were observed; unrun gates. Changed relevant inputs invalidate affected evidence; no timestamp-only freshness rule.>

## Evidence
